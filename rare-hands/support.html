Rare Hands: Hong Kong Mahjong

How do I play?

Drag your finger through neighbouring tiles (across, down or diagonally) to build a mahjong hand: sets of three plus one pair. Rarer hands score more. Settings ▸ Help ▸ How to play replays the lesson, and Hands & scoring lists every pattern with real tiles.

I can't read the Chinese characters

Turn on Settings ▸ Game ▸ Tile numbers. Every tile then shows a small corner index: 1–9 for the suits, E S W N for the winds, C F P for the dragons and J for the joker.

How do I get more power-ups?

Settings ▸ More ▸ Power-ups: watch a short ad to earn one Remove, Refresh or Joker, up to 3 of each per day. With the Ad-free rewards purchase you claim them without ads.

I bought something on another device or reinstalled the app

Open Settings ▸ More ▸ Shop and tap Restore purchases. You need to be signed in with the same Apple ID. Earned power-ups are stored on the device and are not restored.
