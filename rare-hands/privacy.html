Privacy policy

Rare Hands: Hong Kong Mahjong · Last updated 16 November 2026

Rare Hands is a single-player puzzle game. You never create an account, enter a name or an email address, and there is no chat. We run no servers of our own. This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stars and best scores), your power-up balance, which one-time purchases you own, and your settings (sound, vibration, tile numbers, language) are saved only on your iPhone. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them; one-time purchases can be restored from your Apple ID.

Daily game leaderboard

The daily game has a leaderboard of the day's best scores. It is provided by Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies).

  • When you finish a daily game while online, the game signs in anonymously and sends your score for that day. Unity gives the installation a random player ID and a generated display name (for example "SwiftTiger"). You never type a name, and the ID is not connected to your Apple ID, your contacts or your advertising identifier.
  • Other players see the generated name and the score. Nothing else about you is shown.
  • To deliver the service, Unity processes technical information such as your IP address and device model. See Unity's privacy policy.
  • The game also asks Unity which version of the daily rules is current, so that everyone on a leaderboard plays the same board. This request carries the same anonymous ID.
  • The daily game works without a connection; the score is then simply not sent. Levels, missions and Endless never send anything.
  • Each day's leaderboard starts empty at 00:00 UTC. The random ID is stored only on your device: deleting the app removes it, and scores it posted can then no longer be connected to you or your device.

Purchases

Purchases are made and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, name or Apple ID. The game only learns which of its products you own, so it can unlock them.

Ads

The game can show ads: optional ads you choose to watch for a power-up, and ads between levels (removable with the Remove ads purchase). Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

  • Ads are never personalised. The game asks Google for non-personalised ads only, and it never asks to track you across other companies' apps and websites (there is no App Tracking Transparency prompt).
  • To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may process device information such as the device model, operating system, IP address (used for approximate, country-level location), ad interactions and diagnostics. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad loads. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings ▸ More ▸ Privacy choices. If you don't consent, the game works the same; you can still claim power-ups with the Ad-free rewards purchase.

Children

Rare Hands is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. Ad requests are marked as not child-directed.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you ourselves. Leaderboard scores are stored by Unity under a random ID that we cannot connect to a person; deleting the app removes that ID from your device. For data processed by Google for ads, use the Privacy choices option above or contact Google. For purchases, contact Apple.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: see the support page.
